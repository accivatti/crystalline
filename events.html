Weddings & staffed events

Built for a full room.

A wedding, a rehearsal dinner, a company party and a milestone birthday all ask for the same thing: food that holds up and a floor that runs quiet.

We plan the menu with you, staff the kitchen and the floor for your guest count and run the service timeline. You get to be a guest at your own event.

  • Seated dinner. Coursed service for receptions where the meal is the main event, typically 20 to 60 guests.
  • The full room. Buffet or stations built around handmade food, from 50 to 150 guests and beyond.
  • Live-fire reception. Pizza from the oven as the centerpiece, indoors or out.

Private dinners

A long table, cooked for.

Fresh pasta made the way my family has for generations, from pinched-by-hand ravioli to a simple, perfect cacio e pepe. Served family-style, for a table that stays long.

Every dinner is built from scratch around your occasion. Spanish tapas, a Moroccan tagine, or a backyard cookout that happens to be the best one you’ve been to. The food meets the occasion, whether that’s white tablecloths or paper plates by the lake.

Signature experience

Live-fire pizza, wherever you gather.

I bring the whole setup to you. The oven, the fire, the dough, and everything around it, wherever your people are: a backyard, a lakehouse, a vineyard, a rooftop. Every guest gets their own pizza, hand-stretched to order and topped however they like, then pulled straight from the live fire and handed over hot.

Most nights I run the whole thing myself, up to about twenty guests, and bring a team when the count climbs higher. Pizza is the centerpiece, but the menu bends to your night.

How it works

From first call to last plate.

  1. A first conversation

    You tell me about the occasion, the place and the people. I ask questions, and we both find out quickly whether it’s a fit.

  2. The menu and the plan

    We build the menu around your guests and the season, then the staffing around your guest count. Weddings include a tasting.

  3. The day of

    We arrive early and run service from the first plate to the last. You stay with your guests.
